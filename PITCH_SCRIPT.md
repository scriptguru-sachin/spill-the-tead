# Spill the Tea — 90-second pitch

Learning complex topics often feels like reading a manual written for someone who already understands it. The information exists, but it is dense, abstract, and easy to forget.

Spill the Tea turns any topic into a fact-grounded conversation between the concepts themselves. If we learn caching, Redis becomes the speed-obsessed character, PostgreSQL protects durable data, and the application is caught between them. Their personalities are entertaining, but their names, roles, and disagreements remain connected to real facts.

The learner controls the conversation one message at a time or uses autoplay. They can interrupt any character with a doubt, and that question stays attached to the exact message that caused it. After the story, reality checks explain where each analogy stops, a concept map visualizes the relationships, trusted sources provide receipts, and a quiz tests actual reasoning.

The experience then adapts. Quiz history determines the learner's difficulty automatically. If they miss a concept, the system generates a linked follow-up episode focused on that weakness. If they do well, it offers a deeper or practical sequel.

Our React frontend communicates with a Laravel API, PostgreSQL stores learning history and adaptive paths, and Gemini with Google Search grounding powers structured, source-backed lessons.

Spill the Tea does not make education less serious. It makes serious ideas memorable enough to follow, question, and understand.
